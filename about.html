About

AI where your teams work.

Diffuse Systems builds tools for organisations that already own compute and have good reasons to keep their data on site.

Origins

From an open project to an enterprise product.

2024

A peer-to-peer experiment

Diffuse Open began with a simple question. Could connected machines run a model too large for any one of them? The project remains available under AGPL-3.0.

In the field

Teams with hardware of their own

Conversations with universities, clinics and public bodies uncovered a different need. These teams had hardware, access rules and data that had to stay on their network.

Enterprise

A deployment a team can operate

Diffuse Enterprise gives machines an identity and operators a record of changes. The team initially focuses on German-speaking Europe and works in German, English and French.

What we hold to

Four rules we do not bend.

Refuse rather than degrade

A placement that will not fit is refused, with the arithmetic.

And with something to do about each term. There is no flag that overrides a safety check: a flag like that gets used in exactly the situation it exists to prevent.

A message is part of the product

An operator at three in the morning is told what is wrong, in numbers.

And what to do instead. Most of the work in this product is that, and it is not decoration.

Nothing calls home

No activation, no licence heartbeat, no telemetry.

Not a concession to air-gapped sites: a product that phones its vendor to confirm you may use it is what our customers are trying to escape.

Transparent by design

Our threat model states what each protection covers.

Your security team reviews exactly what it gets, in writing, before anything is installed.
