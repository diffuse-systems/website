Research & education

A teaching lab becomes a model lab.

A department already has a room full of workstations. After teaching hours, a research team wants to test a model on its own corpus.

A first experiment

Reserve a time window, choose an approved corpus and compare the base model with an adapter on held-out questions.

What to review

Answer quality and job duration within the reserved window.

Industry & operations

The right manual, next to the machine.

A technician is looking for a procedure across several revisions. Their document assistant needs to cite the right passage in the approved manual.

A first experiment

Select the excerpts in your application and pass them to the model over the local API. Ask a technician to check the reference and revision.

What to measure

Correct references and time to first token.

Professional services

A first draft within the matter’s boundaries.

A consulting team needs to reconcile findings across several documents before a client meeting. It works inside an environment reserved for the matter.

A first experiment

Prepare a synthetic or approved sample. Request a short summary, then ask the matter owner to review citations and omissions.

What to validate

Citation accuracy and user access permissions.
